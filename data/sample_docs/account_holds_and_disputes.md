# Account Holds and Transaction Disputes

## Regulation CC Holds

Under Regulation CC, funds from a deposited check may be held before becoming
available for withdrawal. Standard holds release the first $225 the next business
day, with the remaining balance available on the second business day for local
checks and up to the seventh business day for certain new-account or large-deposit
scenarios.

Exception holds (new accounts open less than 30 days, repeated overdrafts, or
reasonable cause to doubt collectibility) may extend availability up to 11 business
days from the deposit date, with required written notice to the customer.

## Debit Card Dispute Process

Customers have 60 days from the statement date on which an error appeared to
submit a written dispute under Regulation E. The bank must acknowledge the dispute
within 10 business days and complete the investigation within 45 days (90 days for
point-of-sale debit transactions, new accounts, or foreign-initiated transactions).

Provisional credit is issued if the investigation extends beyond 10 business days,
provided the customer has given written consent and the account is in good
standing.

## Stop Payments

A stop payment order on a paper check is valid for 6 months and can be renewed.
Stop payments on ACH debits under a standing authorization must be provided to the
bank at least 3 business days before the scheduled transfer date to be effective.
