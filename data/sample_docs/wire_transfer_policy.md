# Wire Transfer Policy

## Domestic Wire Transfers

Domestic wire transfers initiated before 4:00 PM Eastern Time on a business day are
processed same-day. Transfers submitted after the cutoff, or on a weekend/holiday,
are processed on the next business day.

The standard fee for an outgoing domestic wire transfer is $25. Incoming domestic
wires are not charged a fee. Wire transfer limits for retail customers are capped at
$100,000 per transaction unless a temporary limit increase has been approved by a
supervisor.

## International Wire Transfers

International wire transfers require the receiving bank's SWIFT/BIC code and the
beneficiary's IBAN or account number. Transfers are subject to a $45 outgoing fee
plus any intermediary bank fees, which are not controlled by the originating bank.

International transfers over $10,000 are automatically flagged for compliance
review under anti-money-laundering (AML) procedures and may be delayed up to two
business days pending manual approval.

## Reversals and Recalls

A wire transfer recall request can be submitted within 24 hours of initiation, but
the receiving bank is not obligated to return funds once they have been credited to
the beneficiary's account. Recall requests should be submitted through the wire
operations team with the original transaction reference number.
